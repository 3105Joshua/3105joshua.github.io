
## Projects

### [Zelda NES Recreation](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/14PK2rdM6xLyD94Inq9-0XGeh9n1H-Hf7)

A recreation of the original NES Legend of Zelda Dungeon 1, developed in Unity for EECS 494.

I worked on the movement system and enemy mechanics for Keese, Stalfos, Goriyas, Wallmasters, Gels, Bladetraps, and Aquamentus.

I also implemented our custom mirror mechanic, as well as the player's health, damage, and knockback mechanics.

My partner and I used GitHub for version control.

### [SplitPoint](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1a2vewdE7YUBIeNPhrvgMaJI98TvouLcE)

A 3D physics-based puzzle game developed in Unity. Players control a ball that can split into a small, fast form and a large, heavy form to solve puzzles.

The game challenges players to use momentum, manipulate pressure plates, navigate tight spaces, and break through obstacles by strategically splitting and merging.
